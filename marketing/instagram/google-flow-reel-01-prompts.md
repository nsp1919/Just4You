# Google Flow Prompt Pack: Just4You Reel 01

## Campaign concept

Five ordinary photos become one unforgettable surprise.

## Marketing goal

Make the product understandable within five seconds, create emotional curiosity, and drive viewers to comment `SURPRISE` for the live demo.

## Recommended Flow setup

- Create one Flow project named `Just4You Reel 01`.
- Select **Video** and **9:16 portrait**.
- Use **Veo 3.1 Fast** for inexpensive drafts, then regenerate selected clips with **Veo 3.1 Quality**.
- Generate each clip at **6 seconds**.
- Request two variations per prompt when credits allow.
- Use **Frames** for clips 2, 3, 4 and 6.
- Use **Text to Video** for clips 1 and 5.
- Keep generated dialogue and generated text disabled. Add the supplied voiceover, captions, logo and CTA during editing.
- Add the six chosen clips to Flow Scenebuilder in numerical order.

## Safety and privacy

Only upload the fictional demo assets listed below. Do not upload customer photos, private celebration pages, real messages, account screens, order data, API keys or analytics exports to Flow.

## Fictional visual identity

Use this identity whenever hands or a device appear:

> The same pair of graceful feminine hands belonging to a fictional young Indian adult, medium-brown skin, short natural nude nails, one thin rose-gold ring on the right index finger. Matte graphite smartphone with rounded corners. No face or identifiable person appears.

## Shared style block

Keep this visual direction in every clip:

> Premium emotional gifting advertisement for an Indian audience. Romantic but modern, intimate and believable. Warm late-afternoon window light, ivory, blush pink and muted rose-gold palette, realistic skin and materials, shallow depth of field, restrained highlights, soft film grain, smooth deliberate camera movement. Vertical 9:16 composition with the main action inside the central 70 percent. Leave the top 15 percent and bottom 20 percent visually quiet for captions and Instagram controls. Photorealistic commercial cinematography, 30 fps, natural motion blur. No spoken dialogue, no generated captions, no generated logos, no watermark.

## Negative block

Append this to every prompt:

> Avoid distorted fingers, extra fingers, duplicate hands, warped phone edges, floating objects without physical logic, illegible text, random letters, fake app logos, fake brand names, visible faces, exaggerated crying, wedding ceremony imagery, cluttered backgrounds, purple neon lighting, hard zooms, whip pans, camera shake, excessive bokeh, surreal transformation, cartoon rendering, split screen, borders, subtitles and watermarks.

## Clip 1: Five photos hook

**Mode:** Text to Video  
**Length:** 6 seconds  
**Purpose:** Pattern interrupt and immediate product setup.

### Flow prompt

```text
Create a six-second vertical 9:16 premium emotional gifting advertisement.

Top-down macro shot of a clean ivory desk in warm late-afternoon window light. The same pair of graceful feminine hands belonging to a fictional young Indian adult, medium-brown skin, short natural nude nails, one thin rose-gold ring on the right index finger, quickly places five small printed photographs around a matte graphite smartphone. The photographs show only abstract celebration details such as flowers, cake, joined hands and warm lights, with no readable text and no identifiable faces.

For the first three seconds, each photograph lands rhythmically on the desk, one after another, with realistic paper movement. During the next three seconds, the right hand gathers the five photographs into a neat stack and slides them towards the smartphone. End with the stack touching the lower edge of the phone, ready for a match cut into the digital surprise.

Camera remains overhead with a very slow controlled push-in. Keep the phone screen dark and reflection-free so a real product screen can be composited later. Keep the centre clear and leave quiet space at the top and bottom.

Premium emotional gifting advertisement for an Indian audience. Romantic but modern, intimate and believable. Warm ivory, blush pink and muted rose-gold palette, realistic materials, shallow depth of field, restrained highlights, soft film grain, smooth motion, natural motion blur, 30 fps.

No spoken dialogue, no generated captions, no generated logos, no watermark. Avoid distorted fingers, extra fingers, duplicate hands, warped phone edges, floating objects without physical logic, illegible text, random letters, fake app logos, visible faces, cluttered backgrounds, hard zooms, whip pans, camera shake, surreal transformation, cartoon rendering, split screen, borders and subtitles.
```

**Length:** 6 seconds

```text

The motion should feel premium, calm and romantic, like a polished mobile product commercial. Keep the central title fully readable. Leave the top 15 percent and bottom 20 percent clear enough for editor-added captions.

No spoken dialogue, no new text, no generated captions, no new logo and no watermark. Avoid changing any letters, names or UI elements. Avoid warped typography, morphing flowers, excessive petals, camera shake, fast zooms, neon colours, visible faces, split screen and borders.
```

## Flow selection checklist

Choose the variation that passes all of these checks:

- The product is visible by second six.
- Hands have five natural fingers and stable jewellery.
- Phone edges remain straight and consistent.
- Uploaded UI text is not rewritten or distorted.
- Camera motion is slow enough for mobile viewers to understand the shot.
- The subject remains inside the central 70 percent.
- There is clear space for captions and Instagram controls.
- No generated watermark, random text or fake app logo appears.
- The visual style remains ivory, blush and rose-gold across all six clips.
