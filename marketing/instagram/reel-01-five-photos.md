# Reel 01: Five Photos Become a Surprise

## Goal

Introduce Just4You in one clear transformation and drive viewers to request the live demo.

## Audience

People aged 18 to 34 planning a birthday surprise for a partner or best friend.

## Duration target

30 to 38 seconds

## Format

Vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920, product screen recording with hands and phone shots. No customer photos or reactions are required.

## Cover text

5 photos. 1 unforgettable surprise.

## Script and shot list

### Hook (0-3s)

**Voiceover:**

This is what five ordinary photos can become.

**Visual:**

Show five generic demo photos dropping rapidly into a phone screen. Cut on each beat.

**On-screen text:**

5 ordinary photos...

### Transformation (3-9s)

**Voiceover:**

Instead of sending another photo collage, turn them into a personalised surprise website.

**Visual:**

Match-cut from the last photo to the Floral live demo opening on a phone. Scroll just enough to reveal the name, message and first animation.

**On-screen text:**

...become their own surprise website

### Build (9-18s)

**Voiceover:**

Add their name, your message, music and the celebration date. Then choose a theme that feels like them.

**Visual:**

Use four fast screen recordings from the creation flow: recipient name, message, music and theme selection. Keep taps synchronised with the named features.

**On-screen text:**

Name + message + music + theme

### Reveal (18-28s)

**Voiceover:**

On their special day, send one private link. It opens with your photos, music, heartfelt words and wishes from everyone they love.

**Visual:**

Show the link arriving in a staged WhatsApp chat, then tap it. Show the countdown or reveal, photo gallery and guest wishes wall in three quick cuts.

**On-screen text:**

Send one link on their special day

### Offer and CTA (28-36s)

**Voiceover:**

It starts at just ₹199 with one year of hosting. Comment SURPRISE and I will send you the live demo.

**Visual:**

End on the strongest full-page demo shot, then a clean Just4You logo and CTA screen.

**On-screen text:**

From ₹199 | 1 year hosting

Comment SURPRISE for the demo

## Audio direction

Use a warm instrumental track with a distinct beat for the first five photo cuts and a gentle lift at the website reveal. Keep music at 10 to 15 percent under the voiceover.

## Editing notes

- Keep every shot between 0.7 and 2.5 seconds.
- Put captions in the centre-safe area so Instagram controls do not cover them.
- Use large high-contrast text, with no more than eight words on screen at once.
- Show the product within the first five seconds.
- Use generic demo media only. Do not expose real customer names, photos, messages, links or account details.
- Avoid a fake reaction shot. Add a real reaction only after receiving explicit publication consent.
- End on the CTA for at least two seconds.

## Asset checklist

- Create-flow screen recordings
- Staged WhatsApp message using fictional details
- Transparent Just4You logo
- Live demo URL ready for manual DM replies

- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Length: 30 to 38 seconds
